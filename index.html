When should the decision change?

DriveWHEN pairs a real nuScenes driving clip with a counterfactual in which one road user is edited. A good driving model should change its action only when the edit makes the old action wrong.

Same kind of edit, different required response

The intervention type alone does not decide the answer: the traffic context does. Each video is labelled separately; the two labels define whether the model should become more conservative, less conservative, or keep its action.

Four interventions on an existing road user

Edits are rendered with CompoSIA on 33-frame front-camera clips. The ego trajectory is kept as recorded.

move-left

A road user on the right moves toward or into the ego lane.

move-right

A road user on the left moves toward or into the ego lane.

forward-away

The lead vehicle in the ego lane accelerates away.

appearance

Only the lead object's appearance changes; position and motion are kept.

How a pair is scored
  • The model sees each video in a separate pass and never sees the intervention type or the paired video.
  • It chooses one of KEEP, SLOW, BRAKE/STOP; three option orders are averaged.
  • PairAcc: both actions exactly correct. DIR-strict: on pairs that require a change, the model's action moves in the required direction.
  • An always-KEEP baseline scores 20.7% PairAcc but 0% DIR-strict, which is why both metrics are reported.
