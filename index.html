DriveWHEN: counterfactual video pairs

Anonymous supplementary material for ICLR 2027 submission #32915. This page contains no information about the authors.

Each pair shows an original nuScenes front-camera clip (33 frames) and its counterfactual rendered with CompoSIA after one controlled intervention on an existing road user. Below each video is the reference ego action. Videos play when clicked; click again to pause. Each model in the paper sees the two videos separately and never sees the intervention type.

InterventionIntended change
move-lefta road user on the right moves laterally toward or into the ego lane
move-righta road user on the left moves laterally toward or into the ego lane
forward-awaythe lead vehicle in the ego lane accelerates away
appearancethe lead object's appearance is replaced; position and motion are kept
